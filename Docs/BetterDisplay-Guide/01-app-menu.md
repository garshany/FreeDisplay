### T01. Menu structure, presentation and navigation — [App Menu](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/app-menu/), [Device menu](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/app-menu/device-menu/), [Tools](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/app-menu/tools/), [Guide home](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/), [User Interface](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/)

- **BetterDisplay behaviour** (Free unless marked Pro): Left-click the status icon opens the App Menu; Option-click opens Settings; right-click opens a distinct native quick menu. Device sections contain immediate adjustments; Tools contains shared actions. Settings retains display/group/application/menu/keyboard preferences and offers Pro/About panes. The home and interface indexes route to these surfaces, integration/Shortcuts/CLI/events/console, licensing, FAQs, tutorials, downloads/community/localization and technical explanations; they do not specify additional implementations of the linked articles. Licensing, purchases, activation, refund/upgrade policies, payment support and early-backer recognition are BetterDisplay-specific.
- Clicking a device header expands/collapses it; collapse and more-options states are remembered. With one listed device, its header is normally hidden and content remains expanded; a setting retains the header. More-options chevrons appear only with assigned content. Default behaviour closes another device's more-options section and hides sibling features when expanding a feature; closing the menu closes expanded features. Preferences can change these behaviours. Popover submenus open on hover; Sidebar expands inline. Capabilities, connection, controller availability, permissions, OS version and menu customization all affect visibility.
- Standard presentation is default. Sidebar uses the screen edge and darkened glass backdrop where macOS 26+ supports it; its buttons stay at bottom. Standard supports top/bottom button placement. Gear opens Settings, Command-comma works when gear is present, ellipsis toggles Tools unless Tools uses its own collapsible header. Quit button defaults hidden. Icon re-click, outside click or Escape closes attached menu without undoing changes/quitting; its own dialogs/submenus must remain usable.
- **Pro:** If detaching is enabled, dragging empty/menu-button background creates a movable ordinary window that survives outside clicks, supports configurable transparency and closes with its close button; it no longer uses Sidebar presentation. Notices show only the highest-priority applicable action: update offer before permission/license notices, with a temporary first-use Settings hint possible. Permission notice opens Keyboard setup; licensing-disabled/trial-days/purchase/outdated-license notices open Pro. Hiding warnings does not fix their causes or hide update notices.

### T02. Device headers, logical connections and recovery — [Header and connection](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/app-menu/device-menu/header-and-connection/)

- **BetterDisplay behaviour** (Free header/identification; Pro physical disconnect/automatic policies): Header shows configured name/icon, main marker, and connected-device sleep-prevention marker. Expanded status distinguishes mirror source/target, incoming/outgoing stream and one/many associations. A virtual receiving screen warns that it receives another desktop. Remembered disconnected entries remain visible; without a switch, display disconnected text. Option-click identifies a connected screen.
- Per-display switch modes are Default/Connect–Disconnect, Screen Control, DDC Power Off, or Disabled. Logical disconnect removes desktop space, not a hardware power command. Screen Control keeps desktop space and uses readable screen/backlight state. DDC power-off may require manual power-on. Hide unsupported/globally hidden/disabled switches; exclusive system virtual-display sessions can disable logical operations. Separate connect/disconnect commands normally disappear when duplicated by the switch, with a menu override to retain them; remain available when the switch has a different function.
- Connection management defaults enabled. DDC screen control warns about compatibility and uncertain wake; main/only physical screen adds confirmation. DDC power-off warns about manual recovery. Proceed acts; Cancel does nothing. Warning suppression persists after proceeding; cancelling clears that warning's dismissal preference. Logical disconnect has its own platform-specific confirmation.
- Apple Silicon disconnect normally removes layout and requests standby; monitor compatibility varies. Failed reconnection may require cable reconnection or closing/reopening the MacBook lid. Default safety preserves an eligible physical screen: app-created and ordinary system virtual displays do not substitute; registered DisplayLink can. Built-in iMac and base-M3 panels require specific overrides. General connection permission does not bypass these. Intel disconnect only approximates sleep, can dim/restore supported brightness, and warns that unplugging/power-off/lid closure while disconnected can strand the desktop until restart; this is reference-only for FreeDisplay's target.
- Auto-connect when no eligible display remains defaults on, needs connection management, and is disabled by the allow-all-disconnected override. Virtual/generic temporary entries do not prevent recovery; registered DisplayLink can. No normal reconnect through a closed lid or exclusive virtual session. Shift-connect requests other physical screens off; Shift-disconnect requests other disconnected physical screens on first. Exclude app virtual and system virtual except Sidecar; retain built-in safeguards. Manually reconnecting auto-disconnected built-in suspends the policy while applicable external hardware remains; manual disconnect or app restart restores it, with notification.
- Virtual connection activates/deactivates a saved definition without deletion. Enforced association connection rules disable manual switches and label the command automatic. Failed creation reports an error. Header sleep notice disables only this device's sleep prevention; others may still hold an assertion. Pending adaptation notice opens device Settings, otherwise an unadapted connected device may show dismissible DDC-configuration notice. Pro covers physical connection management, auto-recovery and built-in auto-disconnect; reconnecting a previously app-disconnected display remains Free. Virtual association feature tiers are not independently specified here.
- **FreeDisplay today**: 🟡 Partial. Names, main highlighting, Option-identification, active/offline switches and reconnect-failure recovery links exist (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:195`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:261`). No switch-mode selection, relationship badges, enforced associations or per-device sleep notice. Disconnect routes through coordinator and maintains offline intent (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:3297`); experimental SkyLight is real disable, public fallback mirrors onto main (`Providers/ExperimentalLifecycleProvider/Sources/ExperimentalLifecycleProvider.swift:10`, `Providers/CoreGraphicsProvider/Sources/CoreGraphicsProvider.swift:106`). **P0:** header describes turning a display off even when fallback only mirrors it (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:227`); it tests active count rather than real recovery eligibility. `Packages/TopologyCore/Sources/TopologyCore/SafetyEngine.swift:43` filters active/nonmirrored/visible candidates but does not exclude virtual classes or encode iMac/base-M3 restrictions. This is a policy gap, not evidence that a physical device was stranded. Auto-disconnect/reconnect and active-surface recovery already exist (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:3525`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:3702`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:3626`); extend these rather than replacing them.
- **Implementation notes**: M–L, High risk for SkyLight/SPIs; public AppKit for identity, IOKit power assertions for sleep. Model connection, backlight and hardware power independently, expose provider semantics, and centralize physical-surface eligibility and recovery. Keep all disable routes behind the same safety authority. Do not promise standby from the public mirror fallback.
- **Acceptance criteria**:
  - With one physical plus one virtual display, default disconnect is rejected; eligible DisplayLink/Sidecar cases follow explicit policy.
  - Main/only-screen and DDC warnings support cancel/suppression correctly; physical standby is never inferred solely from API success.
  - Manual reconnect overrides automatic disconnection for the documented interval; lid-closed/exclusive-session rules hold; virtual disconnect preserves its definition.

### T03. Display-mode selection, filtering and favorites — [Display Mode](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/app-menu/device-menu/display-mode/)

- **BetterDisplay behaviour** (Free selection/filtering; Pro favorites, scale matching and protection): Device > Display Mode, named Virtual Screen Mode for a linked virtual screen; connected source required. Mirror targets need the explicit allow-resolution-change preference. Each row represents logical width/height, aspect or notch, refresh, bit depth and HiDPI/LoDPI; duplicate dimensions can legitimately represent different modes. Mark current, favorite, default/native, unsafe and invalid states. Default/native shortcuts remain outside filters and can collapse to one row if identical.
- Clicking a row or its context-menu switch command applies immediately, without Apply/countdown. Desktop-size changes temporarily close/reopen attached menu, update capture outputs and reapply applicable layout rules. Mode writes are persistent macOS configuration, not a lock. Enabled resolution/rate protection adopts deliberate choices. Group UI-scale matching may affect other displays; Shift-selection bypasses it once.
- Exactly five remembered favorite slots per device. Manage assigns current mode; occupied slots indicate replacement and have remove buttons. Row context menu assigns any other slot, marks occupied slots as replacement, omits a slot already assigned there and removes assignments without switching. Unresolvable favorites retain dimensions and an unavailable state; removal remains possible. Matching uses dimensions, rendering density and rate, with fallback for depth/rate representation: display actual matched tags rather than claiming identical future signal.
- Filter categories: aspect/notch, refresh, depth, density. Defaults follow current aspect/rate/depth and density menu preference. Within each category selected values are OR; categories combine with AND. Click selects one; Shift/Control/Option-click toggles membership. Empty categories may yield no rows. Filter changes never change mode. Reset Default rebuilds from current mode and clears custom filtering/Show All/custom-scaled-only without affecting favorites/current mode. Remember filter and panel state per display; opening Manage closes Filter and vice versa.
- Show All bypasses tags and includes unsafe/invalid received modes, but cannot invent modes; below-720-logical-height remains excluded unless separately enabled globally. Context details report current/default/native/unsafe/invalid labels. Custom-scaled-only appears only with configured custom sizes, includes their rotated forms, retains refresh/depth/density filtering, and is mutually exclusive with Show All. Both CPU families support ordinary selection, subject to actual mode availability.
- **FreeDisplay today**: 🟡 Partial. Immediate tray selection, current/HiDPI/rate/native/default labels, compact/all variants and favorites exist (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:519`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:550`). Selection uses a deliberate keep/revert window (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/TrayPanelController.swift:73`; `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:3081`), while detailed Settings has an Apply workflow (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/DisplayDetailView.swift:89`); retain these safeguards as documented product differences. Missing category filters, context summaries, slot UI, unavailable favorites, custom-scaled view and mirror-target policy. Catalog excludes unusable modes before Show All (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/TrayModeCatalog.swift:30`), so its current label means all retained variants. `DisplayMode.bitDepth` exists but catalog construction leaves it unset (`Packages/DisplayDomain/Sources/DisplayDomain/DisplayModels.swift:33`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/TrayModeCatalog.swift:37`). **P0:** favorite keys round rates to integers, conflating e.g. 59.94/60; absent favorites disappear (`Packages/DisplayDomain/Sources/DisplayDomain/FavoriteResolutions.swift:15`, `Packages/DisplayDomain/Sources/DisplayDomain/FavoriteResolutions.swift:47`). No five-slot limit.
- **Implementation notes**: M for metadata/filter model and stable exact favorite identity; public CoreGraphics enumeration/configuration, persisted Codable records. Do not deduplicate by dimensions/rate alone when depth/rendering differs. Keep unsafe modes opt-in with recoverability; preserve existing rollback.
- **Acceptance criteria**:
  - Fractional/integer rates and different depth/density combinations remain distinguishable; filter modifiers never submit a mode.
  - Save/replace/remove all five slots; unplug/change mode availability; unavailable assignments remain removable and cannot apply.
  - Current/default/native/favorite visibility survives filtering; below-720 and unsafe rules are independent; failed writes reread actual state.

### T04. Main sliders and brightness composition — [Sliders](https://betterdisplay.pro/guide/interface-reference/app-menu/device-menu/sliders/)

- **BetterDisplay behaviour** (ordinary controls Free): Brightness/Resolution default to first level, Volume to more options, Contrast hidden; Menu settings control placement, labels, values and markers. Require connected device and enabled controller. Brightness/contrast/volume update during drag; resolution waits until release plus a brief delay. No Apply button. Remember values subject to each controller's restore/readback policy; the number while adjusting is requested, with hardware latency/discrete steps possible.
- Interior useful-value markers attract the knob; normally visible during interaction, optionally always. Option bypasses snapping. Shift-adjustment or toggle bypasses group sync once without changing the group's policy. Scrolling/swiping defaults off and is supported only for brightness/contrast/volume. Brightness may use a scroll safety floor; drag still reaches its configured range. Resolution never accepts scroll changes.
- Hardware brightness changes the chosen hardware control. Software brightness dims beyond hardware minimum or upscales when supported, with Neutral marking unchanged output. Combined brightness coordinates both in one range, marked Min HW and optionally Max HW before upscaling; optional hardware/software subsliders expose components. Ranges can exceed 100%. Prefer combined, then hardware, when hardware is permitted; otherwise software. Third-party HDR normally bypasses hardware unless HDR/forced-hardware preference allows it. Direct Apple XDR has its own label/range. Percentages are configured control scales, not comparable physical luminance. Nits requires menu and per-device reporting enabled plus controller data; derived values must be distinguished from reported values.
- Prefer software contrast only when explicitly selected and available; otherwise hardware then software fallback. Volume targets the assigned monitor/native-audio/network controller, regardless of current playback device. Icon toggles mute only if supplied. Starting volume adjustment clears mute; muted knob may show minimum while saved level is retained. Mute/zero-volume strategies are configured separately. One optional feedback sound follows drag release when a suitable output exists; scrolling does not trigger it.
- Resolution value is logical desktop size, distinct from render pixels. Percentage compares against default desktop size; magnification compares apparent UI size against native resolution (derived mode size for managed virtuals), reverses direction, keeps Resolution label and uses magnifier icon. Percentage and native-aspect restriction default on, require at least **12** eligible modes; magnification defaults off. Optional dimensions accompany percentages.
- Slider always retains current size; alternatives normally match native aspect, current rate/depth and compatible density plus size limits. Managed virtual screens bypass native-aspect restriction. Exclude unsafe alternatives and normally logical heights below **720**. Omit slider for mirror targets or fewer than two choices. Marker precedence: favorite matching size+density, then current, default, native; no default/native markers on managed virtuals. Shift through mode application bypasses UI-scale matching; Option avoids marker attraction, not discrete modes. Reconfiguration updates protection/layout/capture outputs.
- **Pro:** HDR/XDR/direct upscaling; numbered favorites; custom groups and synchronization beyond default-group basic brightness, including contrast/volume/separate hardware/software; UI-scale matching and layout protection. A specifically Pro-badged slider is disabled without access. Ordinary controllers work on both CPU families; color-table upscaling requires Apple Silicon, Intel needs compatible overlay. Direct XDR needs compatible built-in presets/control, not merely a processor family.
- **FreeDisplay today**: 🟡 Partial. Live brightness with native/DDC/software routing, separate dimming, DDC volume/mute and resolution-on-release exist (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:294`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:331`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:358`; `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:1280`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/AppModel.swift:1374`). XDR toggle uses a Metal EDR trigger plus gamma boost (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/XDRBrightnessController.swift:14`), not BetterDisplay direct preset control. No hardware+software combined mapping, nits, configurable placement, scroll, markers/modifier bypass, magnification or feedback release hook (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/MenuBarView.swift:1374`). “Combined” dimming means gamma+overlay, not hardware+software. Brightness/contrast group sync exists, with learned offsets rather than Shift bypass. **P0:** resolution compact scoring prefers native/default and HiDPI before matching rate; slider filtering only enforces aspect, so a size drag can silently change refresh/density (`Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/TrayModeCatalog.swift:83`, `Apps/OpenDisplay/Sources/TrayModeCatalog.swift:93`).
